About

A trusted partner to program leadership.

Ling Shen (Wendy) is an experienced PMO professional who supports large-scale programs from governance through to go-live. She coordinates Steering Committees and program boards, turns multi-workstream delivery into clear reporting, and keeps RAID, schedule and benefits information current so leaders can decide with confidence.

  • Executive governance

    Steering Committee packs, board reporting and decision logs that are concise, accurate and on time.

  • Integrated delivery

    Schedules, dependencies and RAID that give one view of progress across complex, multi-workstream programs.

  • Stakeholder clarity

    Translating technical and delivery detail into information executives, business teams and vendors can act on.
